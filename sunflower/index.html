Kansas · Data-center land

Sites with power & gas.

How scoring works

Kansas Certified Sites and public LOIS listings (Kansas Commerce, Evergy, Greater Wichita Partnership) of 100+ acres; smaller sites are excluded, and a suspect acreage is kept only if another source shows 100+ acres, ranked 0–100 overall: 60% infrastructure (high-voltage transmission, substations, gas pipelines, scale, fiber, water) and 40% community (45% laws & zoning, 30% local news about the site’s own city/county, 25% the local economic development org’s data-center/tech marketing and incentives, all with cited sources). Each site also shows whether it is inside city limits. Tap a site for the breakdown, every cited issue and who to call.

Kansas · Self-storage · 6 largest cities

Storage sites & facilities.

How scoring works

Development land of 4–12 acres (5–10 is the target; anything outside is flagged) and existing self-storage facilities for sale in Wichita, Overland Park, Kansas City KS, Olathe, Topeka and Lawrence. Each row is scored 0–100: demand 30 (3-mile population, growth, income, renters, permits, lakes), competition 25 (self-storage SF per person within 3 mi), visibility 15 (KDOT traffic count on the frontage road), zoning 20 (by right / conditional / not permitted, code cited) and price 10. Tap a row for the breakdown, format fit, sources and who to call.
